# Atto staking rules {#staking}

<StakingContent>

Staking is Atto's reward mechanism for eligible accounts delegated to participating [voters](/explorer/voters). Delegation also assigns voting weight for Open Representative Voting, but staking rewards are separate from transaction consensus.

Try the [staking overview and reward calculator](/staking) to see how the calculation works with your balance.

:::note Reward rates can change
Staking rewards are variable and depend on the current reward policy, account eligibility, and voter settings. This page also shows the base-APY × GSI programme calculation. Displayed APY and payout timing can change.
:::

**Programme inputs:** The displayed base APY is **<Metric name="distribution.staking.annual-percentage-yield" suffix="%" />**, the [GSI](/docs/growth-stability-index) is **<Metric name="distribution.growth-stability-index" suffix="" />**, and the effective programme APY is **<Metric name="distribution.staking.annual-percentage-yield.effective" suffix="%" />** (base APY × GSI). The effective figure is before the selected voter's delegator share and any cap-related reduction, so an eligible account's rate may be lower. These figures are not a promised user return, and ATTO's market value may fall.

## Why Stake?

- **Receive staking rewards**: Eligible accounts may receive rewards based on the displayed variable APY
- **Support representative diversity**: Choosing among voters can distribute delegated voting weight
- **Keep control of your funds**: Delegation assigns voting weight without transferring custody

## How to Start Staking

1. **Get Atto**: Under current rules, have at least **$10 USD worth** of Atto in your wallet to meet the displayed minimum
2. **Choose a Voter**: In your wallet, select a participating voter that displays an APY percentage ([browse active voters](/explorer/voters))
3. **Stay Eligible**: Make at least one transaction every 30 days
4. **Receive Funds**: Log into your wallet regularly to receive pending transactions so they can count toward your balance

## How Rewards Are Calculated

### Effective Balance

Reward calculations use your **effective balance**:

- The **average of your daily balances** over the past 30 days
- Capped at your **current balance** (you can't earn on funds you no longer hold)

This rewards consistent holders and prevents gaming the system by briefly inflating balances.

### Pre-Share Illustration

The simplified expression below illustrates the amount before applying voter sharing and any cap-related reduction. Its inputs and eligibility rules may change.

```
Illustrative Pre-Share Amount = Effective Balance × Programme Daily Rate
```

The programme daily rate is derived from the programme annual APY. This is not a complete user payout formula: the selected voter's delegator share applies, and allocations may then be proportionally reduced when that voter's delegated balance exceeds the cap.

### Voter Cap

The current reward calculation applies a **cap of 180 million Atto** to each voter's total delegated balance. Above the cap, reward allocations are proportionally reduced for that voter's eligible delegators.

**Tip:** A participating voter with lower total delegation may be less affected by the cap.

### GSI Influence

The displayed calculation multiplies base APY by the [Growth Stability Index (GSI)](/docs/growth-stability-index). For example, a GSI of 0.5 halves the base rate; a GSI of 2 doubles it. Your account's reward also depends on voter sharing, the delegation cap and eligibility. The FAQ below explains the distinction between this calculation and the programme's active settings.

## Payout Schedule

Under current operations, the reward system generally processes staking rewards once per day for eligible accounts using the previous day's calculation. Processing time may vary.

# Mining

Folding@Home uses participants' computing power for **medical research**, and eligible participants may qualify for Atto mining rewards.
This work is separate from Atto's ORV consensus, which uses representative voting rather than competitive proof-of-work mining. Each Atto
transaction still includes lightweight anti-spam work.

By putting your computing power to good use, we create a fair and meaningful way to **distribute attos** to supporters of valuable causes.

:::note Mining reward disclosure
Folding@Home participants may receive Atto under the current reward policy. Eligibility, rates, calculations, timing, and continued availability are variable.
:::

Here’s how to get started:

## Installation

### Step 1: Install Folding@Home

If you haven’t already, download and install Folding@Home.

#### Mac & Windows

Download the client from [folding@home](https://foldingathome.org/start-folding/) and follow the installation instructions.

#### Ubuntu & Debian

```bash
wget https://download.foldingathome.org/releases/public/fah-client/debian-10-64bit/release/fah-client_8.4.9_amd64.deb
sudo dpkg -i --ignore-depends=policykit-1 fah-client_8.4.9_amd64.deb
```

To ensure the Folding@Home client starts automatically:
```bash
sudo systemctl enable --now fahclient
```

### Step 2: Create an Account

Go to: https://v8-4.foldingathome.org

Fill out the following fields:
1. Email
2. Passphrase (and Confirm Passphrase)
3. Username — use your Atto account **without** the `atto://` prefix
4. Team — enter `1066107`

<br />
<br />

:::danger
Do not send miner rewards to an exchange deposit address!
- Exchange deposit addresses are for personal deposits only, mining payouts are not supported.
- Rewards sent directly from the miner may not be credited and can be permanently lost.
- Repeated mining payouts to an exchange can trigger risk controls; your account may be restricted or locked.
:::

:::warning
You can change your username and team later, but if you start mining, **incorrect values will prevent you from receiving rewards** for completed work units.
:::

### Step 3: Add a Passkey (Optional but VERY Recommended)

A passkey is a unique 32-character token that links your work to your Folding@Home username. It unlocks the Quick Return Bonus (QRB), which can boost your points by 2× to over 10× if you consistently return work units on time with a success rate of 80% or higher.

#### How to get your passkey (takes less than 30 seconds):

1. Visit [apps.foldingathome.org/getpasskey](https://apps.foldingathome.org/getpasskey)
2. Enter your Folding@Home username and email address
3. Click **Get Passkey** – the token will be emailed to you
4. Copy the 32-character passkey from the email

#### Add it to your account

Click the gear icon at the top right:
<IdealImage img={require('./click-configuration.png')} alt="Screenshot showing where to click to configure your Folding@Home account." />

Paste your passkey and save:
<IdealImage img={require('./add-passkey.png')} alt="Screenshot showing where to add your Folding@Home passkey." />

:::warning
Bonus points apply only if you **use a passkey**, complete **10 successful** bonus-eligible WUs (to activate) and
maintain an overall **≥80%** successful WU return rate. **Partial returns do not earn bonus points.**
:::


### Step 4: Start folding

Now it's time to shine! Open the Folding@Home interface and click **Fold All** to begin contributing.
Your computer will start working on a unit of research, which may take some time to complete depending on your hardware and system settings.

You can monitor progress, adjust CPU and GPU usage, and choose whether Folding@Home runs only when your computer is idle or continuously.

:::tip
To maximize your contribution, consider running Folding@Home continuously. You can also enable GPU folding (if supported) to significantly boost your output.
:::

When your username and team are correctly configured, reported points can be tracked for the mining reward calculation.

## How Rewards Are Distributed

The reward system periodically checks Folding@Home scores and may distribute rewards for **completed work units** to eligible participants.

For each distribution cycle, the calculation uses the time since the previous update. The emission rate and cycle timing may change:

* **Emissions are allocated per minute at a variable rate `E`** under the distribution policy.
* If `M` minutes have passed since the previous update, the cycle allocation is `E × M` attos.
* This allocation is then **split proportionally** among eligible team members based on the points they contributed during that period, as reported by the Folding@Home stats API.

For example, an eligible participant whose machine provided 10% of the covered team contribution would be allocated 10% of that cycle's mining reward pool under this calculation.

Your Atto address (used as your Folding@Home username) is what links your contribution to your wallet—make sure it's accurate!

### Distribution Policy

The published [Growth Stability Index (GSI)](/docs/growth-stability-index) design can scale configured distribution rates in response to market data. Whether mining is currently mapped to that mechanism, together with the applied settings and cadence, must be confirmed from current operational records. Reward settings remain variable under the distribution policy.
